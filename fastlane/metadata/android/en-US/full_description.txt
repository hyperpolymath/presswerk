Presswerk (Print Doctor) is a local-first print router. It discovers IPP printers via mDNS, lets you print PDFs and images from your device, and records a complete audit trail — all without a cloud account.

• No analytics, no ads, no sign-in. Files stay on-device.
• Form-fill provenance (FFP): machine-filled forms are distinguished from hand-filled ones and shown on the Advanced Jobs page; blank-print hazards are flagged there.
• Works offline on your local network.

Privacy: https://hyperpolymath.dev/presswerk/privacy
